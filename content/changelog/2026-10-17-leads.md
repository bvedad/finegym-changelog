People who are interested in your gym but haven't bought anything yet now have their own page, **Leads**, under Members. It's on every plan.

**Leads arrive on their own.** Share your new enquiry form, on the **Contact** tab of your gym site or embedded on your own website, and every visitor who sends it becomes a lead. You get an email straight away. Someone who signs up on your gym site without buying appears there too, and you can add a lead yourself for someone who called or walked in.

**Their progress updates itself.** A lead moves from **New** to **Trying it out** when they book a class or start a free plan, and to **Became a member** when they start a paid membership, wherever they do it. Mark leads **Contacted** or **Not interested** yourself.

**Nobody falls through the cracks.** Give each lead an owner and a follow-up date. **Follow-ups due** shows who to get back to today, and **Assigned to me** shows only yours. On the Growth plan, the **Follow up new leads** automation can do the writing for you.

Read more in the [leads guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/leads).
