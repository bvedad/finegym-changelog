---
title: "Set a Default Language for Your Gym"
date: "2026-08-14"
categories: ["Admin", "i18n"]
---

You can now choose a default language for your gym. New members and staff inherit it automatically, and it's used for their emails, notifications, and PDF invoices.

- **Gym-wide default** — Set your preferred language in **Settings → General Settings → Language & Time Zone** (English, Bosanski, Português, or Español)
- **Per-person override** — Each member and staff member can still use their own language; leave theirs on **Gym default** to follow the gym setting
- **Consistent everywhere** — Invoices and their attached PDFs now render in the recipient's resolved language

Read more in the [general settings guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/settings/general-settings#default-language).
