Direct debit now works in three more currencies. A business that bills in British pounds collects by **Bacs Direct Debit**, in Canadian dollars by **pre-authorized debit (PAD)**, and in New Zealand dollars by **BECS Direct Debit**. They join BECS in Australia, SEPA in Europe and ACH in the United States.

**Nothing new to learn.** You turn it on the same way: **Settings → Bank connection** reads **Turned on** once the payment method is active in your Stripe account. Members save their bank account on Stripe's secure form: a sort code and an account number in the UK, their bank's sign-in in Canada, an account number in New Zealand.

**Good to know**

- A Bacs debit takes about a week the first time, while the member's bank sets the Direct Debit up, and about four business days after that. It shows as "Stripe" on the member's bank statement.
- With all three, Stripe emails the member a notice before each debit.

Read more in [Accept direct debit payments](https://docs.finegym.io/payments/accept-direct-debit-payments) and [How direct debit payments work](https://docs.finegym.io/payments/how-direct-debit-payments-work).
