---
title: "Members Can Now Use Finegym in a Browser"
date: "2026-09-06"
categories: ["Members", "Payments", "Scheduling", "Admin"]
---

Until now, a member who wanted to see what they owed, sign a waiver or check when their membership renewed had one route: install the app. That is a lot to ask of someone who joined last week, and it left staff answering questions at the desk that members could have answered themselves.

Every gym's site at `https://<your-gym>.sites.finegym.io` now has a members' area behind a sign-in, alongside the public schedule and plans that were already there.

**Members sign in and get their own account.** Their **bookings**, upcoming and past, with the class or appointment named, who is teaching it, and a Cancel button while cancelling is still allowed. Their **memberships**, with what each one covers, sessions left, the next bill date, and the option to cancel or update the card. Their **invoices**, which they can **pay by card in the browser** — and download as a PDF or receipt afterwards. Their **visits**, grouped by day, showing which membership each one was taken against. Their **documents**, which they can read and **sign with a finger or a mouse**. The **emails and texts** the gym has sent them. And a **check-in QR code** to show at the front desk.

**They can check in from the schedule too.** A class happening today shows a Check in button on the class itself, the same as the app.

**Profile is theirs to manage**: photo — with a cropper, so it is framed rather than whatever the camera caught — name, date of birth, phone, address, guardian details for under-18s, language, text-message preferences, password, and account deletion.

**Invoice emails now link straight to it.** The email a member gets for a recurring membership used to say "open the mobile application to review and pay". It now carries a link to the invoice itself, so paying is a click from the inbox. Text messages carry the same link.

The whole thing is **translated into English, Bosnian, Spanish and Portuguese**, and follows each member's own language choice — including dates and times, so a Bosnian member reads `subota, 5. septembar` and a 24-hour clock.

**Nothing to switch on.** Every gym already has the site; the members' area is simply there now. Share `https://<your-gym>.sites.finegym.io` with your members, or let the invoice emails do it for you.

Read more in the [gym website guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/settings/embed-widgets).
