---
title: "Choose the Appointment Times Clients See"
date: "2026-11-19"
categories: ["Admin", "Scheduling"]
---

Two new settings under **Settings → General settings → Appointment times clients see** decide which of a coach's open times members and website visitors can pick. Staff always see every open time.

**Minimize gaps.** Once a coach has something on a day, clients are offered only the times right before or after it, so sessions stay together instead of scattered through the day. **Gaps allowed** can let longer gaps through, of at least 1 to 6 hours. A day is never closed by it: when the times beside a booking can no longer be booked, clients are offered the nearest one that can.

**Look busy.** Clients see only part of each day's open times, with 10% to 90% of them hidden, so a new schedule looks fuller. Booked times count as hidden, so the number of times on offer stays the same until the day really fills up.

Both cover every appointment type, in the member app, on your website and at the front desk kiosk. Clients can't see the settings.

Read more in [Choose the appointment times clients see](https://docs.finegym.io/schedule/appointment-times-clients-see).
