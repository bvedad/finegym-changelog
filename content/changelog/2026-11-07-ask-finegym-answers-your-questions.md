---
title: "Ask Finegym Answers Your Questions, About Finegym and About Your Business"
date: "2026-11-07"
categories: ["Admin"]
---

The **Help** menu in the admin now opens **Ask Finegym**, a panel that answers your questions while you keep working.

**It knows the help center.** Ask how to do something ("How do I freeze a membership?") and you get the steps, with the pages they mention as links you can click, and the articles the answer came from under **Read more**.

**It knows your business.** Ask about a member ("Can Ana book tomorrow?", "Is Jon paid up?"), your lists ("Who is ending soon?", "Who owes money?"), your numbers ("How did October go compared to September?"), the schedule, your plans and class types, which automatic emails are on, your staff, your leads, or what happened today. It reads your own records to answer, and sees only what you can see: money needs the Sales, Refunds or Reports permission, and a coach gets their own classes. It never changes anything.

**You watch it work.** The panel says what it is reading ("Looking at members…", "Adding up the numbers…") and the answer appears as it is written. Ask a follow-up in the same chat, come back to it from **Recent chats**, attach or paste a screenshot of the screen you're asking about, and rate each answer with a thumb up or down. When the help center doesn't cover a question, it says so and offers **Contact support**, with your chat attached.

**Contact support, from where you are.** The Help menu's **Contact support** opens a form in place of the copied address. Your message reaches us with the page you were on, your browser and what failed, so we can start on the answer, and we reply to your sign-in email.

**A dot when there's news.** The Help button shows a dot when this changelog has an entry you haven't seen, and names the newest one under **What's new**.

Read more in [Ask Finegym a question](https://docs.finegym.io/getting-started/ask-finegym) and [Contact support](https://docs.finegym.io/getting-started/contact-support).
