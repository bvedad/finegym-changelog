---
title: "Remind Members Before Their Membership Ends"
date: "2026-10-11"
categories: ["Admin", "Memberships", "Email"]
---

A membership that quietly runs out is a member you have to win back. Finegym now reminds them first.

**An email before the end date.** Five days before a non-recurring membership, an expiring punch card or a recurring membership with a fixed end runs out, the member gets an email naming the membership and the date, with a link straight to that plan's checkout on your gym site. The new membership is set to start the day after the current one ends, so renewing early never pays twice for the same days. If the plan is no longer sold online, the link opens your plan list instead. Where messaging is on, the same reminder goes out by SMS or WhatsApp.

**Your number of days.** Change how far ahead the reminder goes under **Settings → General → Member retention**, anywhere from 1 to 90 days. The wording, the send time and the on/off switch are under **Settings → Email Templates → Membership Expiring Soon**, like every other email.

**Nobody is nagged.** Memberships shorter than two weeks, such as trials, get no reminder, and neither do canceled or frozen memberships.

Read more in the [general settings guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/settings/general-settings#membership-expiring-soon-reminder).
