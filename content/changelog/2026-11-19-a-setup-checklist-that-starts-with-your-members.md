The **Set up your business** checklist on Today has a new order, and two new things on its steps.

**Members first.** The list starts with the three steps that get a business running: add your first member, create a membership plan, and the new **Give members their memberships**. Then your first class, your staff, card payments and your logo.

**Skip what isn't for you.** Three steps carry a line under them: **I only offer appointments**, **I work alone**, **I don't use Stripe**. A skipped step counts as done for your whole business, and **Undo** brings it back.

**Watch it being done.** **Lesson** at the end of a step plays the Academy lesson that shows it, and **Watch the 2-minute tour** at the top of the list shows Today, the checklist and the rest of the app.

Read more in [Set up your business](https://docs.finegym.io/getting-started/set-up-your-business) and [Your first week with Finegym](https://docs.finegym.io/getting-started/your-first-week).
