---
title: "Ask Members Your Own Questions"
date: "2026-10-17"
categories: ["Admin", "Members"]
---

Collect details such as an emergency contact or medical needs straight from your members, instead of typing them in yourself.

**Any custom field can be a question.** Tick **Ask members** on a custom field, and members answer it themselves on your gym site: when they sign up, right after they set up their account, and on their profile, where they can change their answers at any time. Parents answer for each child in their family.

**The usual questions in one click.** **Add common questions** adds an emergency contact (name, phone and relationship) or a question about medical conditions or dietary needs, ready to use. A new **Long text** field type fits longer answers, and arrows set the order members see the questions in.

**Required answers come in.** A member who hasn't answered a required question, for example one you added after they joined, is asked for it on their account page until they do. It never stops them from booking or buying.

**Only staff see the answers**, in the Custom Fields section of the member's profile. The Finegym app doesn't ask these questions yet; members answer them on your gym site.

Read more in the [custom fields guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/settings/custom-fields#questions-members-answer).
