---
title: "Replies to Your Emails Reach You"
date: "2026-11-13"
categories: ["Admin", "Improvements"]
---

Every email your business sends, an invoice, a booking confirmation, a reminder, goes out from Finegym's address with your business's name on it. Until now a member who hit reply was writing to a mailbox nobody reads.

**Replies come to you.** A reply to any of these emails now arrives at your **Customer service email**, the address under **Settings → General settings → Customer service**. Point it at an inbox someone watches: "Can I move my session to Thursday?" lands there, not in the void. Emails Finegym sends in its own name, such as your welcome and account notices, keep their own reply address.

**Finegym's own emails speak your language, subject line included.** The welcome email, the account notices and the note reminders already came in your language; their subject lines sometimes did not, so an inbox could show an English subject over a Portuguese email. Both match now.

**Searching for a coach while booking an appointment works again.** Typing a coach's name in **Book an appointment** answered "Not found" instead of the coach. Fixed.

Read more in [Business details and time zone](https://docs.finegym.io/settings/business-details-and-time-zone) and [Email templates](https://docs.finegym.io/settings/email-templates).
