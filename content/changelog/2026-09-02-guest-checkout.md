---
title: "Sell to Walk-Ins Without Creating a Member"
date: "2026-09-02"
categories: ["Payments", "Admin"]
---

Someone walks in off the street and wants a bottle of water, a towel, or a day pass. Until now you had to create a member record for them — cluttering your member list with people you'll never see again, and skewing your member count.

Now you can just take the money. When creating an invoice, turn on **Check out as guest** instead of picking a customer:

- **No member record is created** — nothing to clean up afterwards, and your member count stays accurate
- **Pay by cash or card**, exactly like any other invoice
- **Shows as "Guest"** in the customer column of your invoice list
- **Everything else works normally** — line items, discounts, taxes, refunds, and reporting all behave the same, and the money lands in your revenue reports alongside member sales

Since there's no email address on file, no receipt is sent automatically — download the receipt PDF if your customer wants one.

Read more in the [invoices guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/invoices#walk-in-guest-sales).
