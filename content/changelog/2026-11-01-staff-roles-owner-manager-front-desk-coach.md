Permissions used to be a list of pages to tick. They are now named after the job, and most businesses need only pick a role.

**Four roles, and Custom.** The **Owner** holds everything, including the plan, billing and the bank connection. A **Manager** runs the business day to day with every permission but those. **Front desk** checks members in, takes payments and books. A **Coach** sees their own classes and appointments and checks their own attendees in. **Custom** lets you pick permissions one by one when none of the four fits.

**Every screen shows only what the person may do.** A coach opens the dashboard and sees their schedule, not your revenue. Buttons and pages a role cannot use are not there, rather than there and refused.

**Your existing staff kept what they had.** Each person was moved to the role that matches the permissions they already held, or to Custom when none did. Nobody's access changed with the release; have a look at **Staff** to see the role each person landed on.

**My availability and My pay for everyone.** Each staff member manages their own availability and sees their own pay, whatever their role.

**Transfer ownership.** When the business changes hands, the owner can pass ownership to another staff member from the Staff page. The previous owner stays on as a Manager.

Read more in [roles and permissions](https://docs.finegym.io/team/roles-and-permissions), [what coaches can do](https://docs.finegym.io/team/what-coaches-can-do) and [transfer ownership](https://docs.finegym.io/team/transfer-ownership).
