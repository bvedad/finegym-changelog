---
title: "One Visit a Day, and a Way to Undo a Check-In"
date: "2026-09-23"
categories: ["Admin", "Members"]
---

Since check-out went away, a second scan at the door — a member leaving, or a card read twice — could turn into a second visit, and on a limited plan a second credit gone. Two changes today put that right.

**Open gym counts one visit per member per day.** A member's second open-gym check-in on the same day is now rejected with *Already checked in today at 09:12*, so a scan on the way out never inflates your visit reports or burns a session. Class and appointment check-ins are separate: someone who trained in the morning is still marked present at their evening class. When a second session in one day is genuine — a morning and an evening workout — staff record it on purpose: from the member's profile or the **Scan member** window, the rejection becomes a question, **Check in anyway**. The self-service kiosk never offers that, so members alone at the screen cannot double-count themselves.

**A mistaken check-in can be deleted.** Wrong member, duplicate scan, a test at the desk — open the row's **⋯** menu on the **Check-Ins** page or on the member's **Attendance** tab and choose **Delete check-in**. The visit no longer counts, a limited plan gets the session back, and a class booking behind the check-in stays in place, so the right person can be checked in straight away. The removal shows in the member's activity, with who did it.

Nothing changes for bookings: a class or appointment reserved by mistake is still cancelled with **Cancel reservation**.

Read more in the [check-in guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/check-in-methods#check-in-validation-rules).
