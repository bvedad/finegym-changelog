---
title: "Wellhub Members Can Book Your Classes"
date: "2026-10-21"
categories: ["Admin", "Scheduling", "Integrations", "Members"]
---

Gyms that partner with Wellhub (formerly Gympass) managed those visits by hand: classes typed into Wellhub's portal, bookings copied over, check-ins validated one by one so the gym got paid. Finegym now does all of it.

**Your classes in the Wellhub app.** Under **Settings → Integrations → Wellhub**, enter your Wellhub Gym ID and switch on the class types Wellhub members may book. Every upcoming class of those types is published to the Wellhub app, two weeks ahead, with the spots left, the booking window, the cancellation cutoff and the coach. New classes, changed times and deleted classes follow on their own, and a closed day is not bookable there either.

**Bookings land on your schedule.** When a Wellhub member books in the Wellhub app, they appear on the class's attendee list with a **Wellhub** label, taking a spot like anyone else. On their first booking they are added to your members with their Wellhub ID; a member of yours who also has Wellhub is matched to their existing profile. A full class, a closed booking window or a closed day declines the booking in the Wellhub app, and a cancellation there frees the spot here.

**You are paid without lifting a finger.** Wellhub pays for validated visits. The moment a member checks in on the Wellhub app, Finegym validates the visit and marks them present on their booking. Prefer to confirm visits yourself? Switch automatic validation off, and the visit is validated when staff mark the member present. The attendee list shows how far each Wellhub booking got, from booked to validated, with the reason when something went wrong.

Read more in the [Wellhub integration guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/settings/integrations/wellhub).
