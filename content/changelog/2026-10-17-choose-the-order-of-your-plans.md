---
title: "Choose the Order Your Membership Plans Appear In"
date: "2026-10-17"
categories: ["Admin", "Members"]
---

Put your most popular plan first. Members now see your plans in the order you choose, everywhere they can buy one: the member app, your gym site and the check-in kiosk.

**Drag them into place.** On **Membership Plans**, click **Change order**, drag each plan by its handle or move it with the arrows, and click **Save order**.

**New plans start at the top.** Until you change the order, plans are listed newest first, so open **Change order** again after creating a plan to put it where it belongs.

Read more in the [membership plans guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/membership-plan-types#changing-the-order-of-your-plans).
