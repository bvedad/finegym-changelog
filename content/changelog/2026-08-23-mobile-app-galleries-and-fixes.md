date: "2026-08-23"

The latest mobile app update brings photo galleries to classes and appointments, along with a batch of stability fixes.

- **Photos for classes & appointments** — Each class and appointment type can now show a gallery of photos on its detail screen; swipe through them and tap any image to view it full-screen. The browse lists have also been redesigned into cleaner, more compact cards.
- **PDF documents on Android** — Documents now open reliably; fixed a blank screen and crash when viewing PDFs on Android.
- **Stays signed in during outages** — A brief server outage no longer signs you out. The app shows a "reconnecting" overlay and recovers automatically once the connection is back.
- General bug fixes and performance improvements.
