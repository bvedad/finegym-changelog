date: "2026-10-17"
categories: ["Admin", "Payments"]

Finegym already knows what comes in. Now it can hold what goes out too: rent, supplies, utilities, staff pay. The new **Expenses** section, next to Payments, shows what's left.

**Record an expense in seconds.** Add the date, a category, the amount, and optionally what it was for and who you paid. Filter by date and category, search, see the total of what you filtered, and export it to CSV.

**Set up the ones that repeat.** Rent, a fixed salary or a subscription: choose **Every week** or **Every month** and it's recorded on its own from then on. Start it in the past and the missed months are recorded straight away. Change the amount for the ones to come, or stop it, from the **Repeating** tab.

**See income against expenses.** The **Income & expenses** report puts the payments you received, less refunds, next to what you spent, by month or by week. You get the result for the period, the period before for comparison, a chart, and what you spent on most.

**Staff pay straight from payroll.** On a closed pay period, **Record as expenses** adds each person's pay as a Staff pay expense. Pay is never counted twice, even if you reopen the period.

Six categories to start with, in your language, and you can add, rename or archive your own. Expenses are on every plan, Free included, for staff with the new **Expenses** permission.

Read more in the [expenses guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/expenses).
