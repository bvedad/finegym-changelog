---
title: "Closures: Mark the Days You're Closed"
date: "2026-10-07"
categories: ["Admin", "Scheduling", "Members", "Mobile"]
---

Public holidays, a renovation week, a day off: until now the only way to stop bookings on a day you were closed was to delete the classes and hope nobody booked an appointment. **Closures** give the gym a proper "closed" state.

**Add the days, and nothing can be booked on them.** Under **Settings → Closures**, add a date range and an optional reason, such as *Public holiday*. From then on members can't book a class, join a waitlist or find an appointment slot on those days; if they try, they're told the gym is closed, with your reason. Staff bookings are refused the same way, so nobody is promised a session behind a locked door.

**Everyone sees it.** The day is marked **Closed** on your dashboard calendar, in the mobile app's schedule and on your gym website. When you create a repeating class, Finegym shows up front how many of its dates fall on closed days and skips them for you, so holidays stop reappearing in the timetable.

**Classes already on those days are yours to clear.** A closure never deletes anything by itself. The Closures page shows how many upcoming classes and appointments still sit on each closure's days: **Delete them** opens bulk delete with the dates filled in, and **View them** takes you to the appointments so you can cancel each one with the member told as usual.

Read more in the [closures guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/settings/closures).
