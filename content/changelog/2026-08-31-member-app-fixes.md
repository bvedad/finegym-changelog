A round of bug fixes and polish for the member app:

- **Signing documents works again** — members were hitting a "Something went wrong" error when signing a digital document (like a waiver or liability form). Signing now goes through as expected.
- **Profile photos work again** — members can upload and change their profile picture without errors.
- **Tidier schedule cards** — class and appointment cards look cleaner and more consistent, and no longer show a large colored block when a type has no image.
