title: "Enroll Members in a Whole Class Series"
date: "2026-08-28"

Running recurring programs just got a lot faster. Instead of booking a student into every class one by one, you can now enroll them into an **entire recurring series in a single action** — perfect for martial arts belt classes, dance courses, gymnastics terms, and any program where students enroll in a schedule rather than individual sessions.

From any class's **actions menu**, choose **Enroll in series**:

- **Pick the series** — it's pre-filled from the class you opened (class type, start time, coach). Narrow it further by start time, coach, or date range.
- **Enroll through a date** — book every future occurrence, or set a **Through date** to stop at the end of the term (e.g. Tuesdays & Thursdays for 8 weeks).
- **Enroll a whole roster** — add as many members as you like, each with the membership they'll use.
- **Preview first** — see exactly how many enrollments will be created and which occurrences will be skipped (full classes, already-booked, plan restrictions) before you confirm.

Members get **one summary email** listing the classes they were added to — not one email per class — and you can turn it off in **Settings → Email Templates**.

There's a matching **Remove from series** action to take members out of every occurrence at once (with its own summary email).

Read more in the [class reservations guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/class-reservations#enrolling-a-member-in-a-whole-series).
