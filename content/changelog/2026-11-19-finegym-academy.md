title: "Finegym Academy: Learn Finegym in Short Video Lessons"
date: "2026-11-19"
categories: ["Admin"]
---

**Academy** is new in the admin's sidebar: four short courses that show Finegym lesson by lesson, in the real app.

**29 lessons, a couple of minutes each.** *Set up your business* takes a new account to its first members, plans, classes, staff and card payments. *Run your day* covers check-ins, sales, bookings and payments. *Grow your business* is leads, products, discounts, campaigns, automations, reports and documents. *Connect your tools* is online payments, your emails, front desk mode, calendars, your website, texts and door access.

**In your language.** The lessons are recorded in English, Spanish and Portuguese, each with captions, and you get the one your account is set to. Bosnian gets the English recording.

**It keeps your place.** A lesson you finish is marked watched and the next one starts by itself. Everyone on your staff has their own progress, and **Continue where you left off** opens the next lesson. Every lesson links the help-center guide it follows.

**Outside the app too.** The same lessons are on [finegym.io/academy](https://www.finegym.io/academy), and each one sits at the top of its guide in the [help center](https://docs.finegym.io).
