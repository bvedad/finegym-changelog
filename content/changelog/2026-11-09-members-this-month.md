---
title: "Members This Month, and Who They Are"
date: "2026-11-09"
categories: ["Admin", "Members"]
---

The Free plan's limit counts everyone who used your business in the last 30 days. Until now the admin called that number "active members", the same words the Members page uses for people who hold a membership today, and the two numbers rarely matched. A business with 31 members on a plan and 18 day-pass regulars saw 31 in one place and 49 in the other.

**The plan's number has its own name.** My plan, the notice at the top of the desk and the plan cards now say **Members this month**: everyone with a current membership, or a check-in or booking in the last 30 days. The Members page's **Active** view keeps meaning what it always did.

**And its own list.** On the Free plan, Members has a **Members this month** view with the same count as My plan. Each row says why the person counts: **Membership**, **Checked in** or **Booked**. The desk notice links straight to it, so when the count is close to 50 you can see who it is made of and who is about to drop off.

Read more in [Finegym plans and billing](https://docs.finegym.io/getting-started/finegym-plans-and-billing).
