---
title: "The Check-In Window Now Works the Same for Every Member"
date: "2026-10-01"
categories: ["Admin", "Members", "Improvements"]
---

When staff clicked **Check in**, the result depended on the clock. A member with one booking starting within the hour was checked in to it without a question. A member whose booking was later that day was checked in to open gym. The **Check in to** window appeared for some members and not for others, with no visible reason.

**Staff check-ins no longer guess from the time.** The **Check in to** window now opens whenever the member has a booking today they can check in to, whatever time it starts, or more than one membership they can use. Choose the class, the appointment or **Open gym**, and nothing is recorded until you confirm. A member with one membership and no booking today is still checked in to open gym straight away.

**The kiosk chooses nothing for the member.** A booking starting within the hour is no longer preselected, and neither is a class the member has just reserved: it appears under **Booked sessions**, where they tap it and confirm. A member who reserves tonight's class at midday can no longer check in to it with a single tap.

Read more in the [check-in guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/check-in-methods#choosing-what-to-check-in-to).
