---
title: "See Why a Class Isn't Offered at Check-In"
date: "2026-10-17"
categories: ["Admin", "Members", "Improvements"]
---

Two members on similar plans could see different classes in the **Check in to** window, and staff had no way to tell why. A class the member's plans didn't include simply wasn't there.

**Every class today, with the reason.** The staff check-in window now also lists, grayed out, the classes the member can't be booked into right now. **Not in their plan** means none of their current memberships includes that class type. **No usage available** means a membership includes it, but its visits for this period are used up.

**Fix it where it's set.** If a class should be included, add its type to the plan under **Certain class types**. The change applies straight away to everyone on that plan.

**The kiosk stays simple.** Members checking themselves in still see only the classes they can book.

Read more in the [check-in guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/check-in-methods#classes-the-members-plans-dont-include).
