---
title: "Parents Pay and Sign for Their Children"
date: "2026-10-19"
categories: ["Members", "Mobile", "Payments"]
---

A child added as a dependent has no login of their own, so until now a parent could book for them but not see their invoices or sign their waivers. Whoever manages a family can now look after each relative's whole account, from the app and from your gym's website.

**Pick who you're looking at.** Invoices, Documents and Memberships show one person at a time: tap **You** or a child at the top. The Family screen links straight to each child's pages, and Home lists what each of them still has to sign or pay.

**Pay their invoices.** A parent opens a child's invoice, sees its items and payments, and pays it with their own card. The payment is recorded on the child's invoice as made by the parent.

**Sign their documents.** A parent reads the child's copy, filled in with the child's details, and signs it for them. The signature is stored on the child and records who signed: your staff see **Signed by** and the parent's name, and the signed PDF says it was signed on the child's behalf.

**Follow their memberships.** A parent sees each child's plans and what's left on them. Buying and canceling stay with the plan's holder and with you.

Siblings who share their mother's email no longer need a second address: one profile keeps the email and manages the family, and the others are added as dependents.

Read more in the [family memberships guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/families).
