---
title: "Members Sign Before They Pay"
date: "2026-11-01"
categories: ["Members", "Payments"]
---

A waiver that is signed after the money has changed hands is a weak waiver. When a document is set to require signing, Finegym now collects the signature first.

**On your website.** A member buying a plan or a drop-in, or booking a class, reads and signs the document before the payment step. Someone who declines pays nothing and books nothing.

**At the desk.** Checking a member in to an appointment waits for the signature the same way class check-in already did. The front desk sees what is unsigned and can hand the member the screen to sign on the spot.

**A new version can ask everyone again.** When you publish a new version of a document, you choose whether existing signatures still count or whether everyone has to sign the new text. Use the second for changes that matter legally, the first for a typo.

**The Signed count means it.** A document's Signed count includes only signatures that still count under the current version, so you can trust it when you check who is covered.

**Family members are covered.** A parent signs on a child's behalf, and the signature says so, as before.

Read more in [documents and waivers](https://docs.finegym.io/settings/documents-and-waivers) and [documents at check-in](https://docs.finegym.io/front-desk/documents-at-check-in).
