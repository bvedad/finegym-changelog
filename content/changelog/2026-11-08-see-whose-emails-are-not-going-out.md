---
title: "See Whose Emails Are Not Going Out"
date: "2026-11-08"
categories: ["Admin", "Members"]
---

When an email to a member can't be delivered because the address doesn't exist, Finegym stops sending to that address, so the next invoice or booking confirmation doesn't bounce again. Until now that happened quietly, and a member could go months without a single email while the admin looked normal. Now you can see it.

**A view for it.** **Members** has a new **Can't be emailed** view, with a count. It lists members whose address bounced and members who have no address at all, such as a child with no family manager to write to. Each row says which: **Email bounces** or **No email address**.

**A banner on the profile.** Opening such a member shows a red banner with the date nothing has reached them since and what the receiving server answered, for example "mailbox not found". **Edit details** takes you to the address. Correct it, and emails go out to the new address from then on. There is nothing else to do: in every bounce we checked, the mailbox simply did not exist.

**Also in this release**

- **Adding a member needs no date of birth.** It's optional now, and can be added later.
- **The owner's Account settings** no longer offers a **Delete account** that refuses: it points to **Business settings**, where the business is deleted or handed to someone else.

Read more in [Find and filter members](https://docs.finegym.io/members/find-and-filter-members) and [A member didn't get an email](https://docs.finegym.io/troubleshooting/member-didnt-get-an-email).
