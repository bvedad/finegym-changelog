---
title: "Image Galleries for Classes & Appointments"
date: "2026-08-23"
categories: ["Admin", "Scheduling", "Members"]
---

You can now showcase your classes and appointment types with more than one photo. Add a set of images to each class type and appointment type, and they'll appear on your public booking pages so members can see what to expect before they book.

- **Multiple photos** — Upload several images per class type or appointment type, instead of a single cover photo
- **Drag to reorder** — Arrange the images in any order; the first image is used as the cover
- **On your booking pages** — Galleries appear on your gym website's schedule and appointment widgets, with a full-screen viewer members can swipe through

Add images under **Schedule → Class Types** or **Appointments → Appointment Types** when creating or editing a type.

Read more in the [class types guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/class-types).
