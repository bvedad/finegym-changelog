---
title: "Everything on Your Business's Clock"
date: "2026-11-01"
categories: ["Admin", "Mobile", "Scheduling"]
---

A class at 6 PM is at 6 PM in your business's time zone, wherever the person looking at it happens to be. That was not always true: a manager traveling, a coach on holiday or a member abroad could see times shifted to their device's clock.

**Every time, on your clock.** The calendar, bookings, check-ins, invoices, reports and exports all use the business's time zone. The member app shows class times on the business's clock too, so a member on a trip still sees when the class actually starts at home. The app update is on its way to the stores.

**You are told when you are elsewhere.** When your computer's clock is in a different zone, the top bar of the dashboard names the business's zone, so there is no guessing which clock a time belongs to.

**Dates and times in your own format.** Each staff member sees dates and times the way their language and region write them; the zone stays the business's.

**Set or change the zone.** The owner sets the time zone under **Settings → General settings**. New businesses choose it at sign-up, from their country.

Read more in [business details and time zone](https://docs.finegym.io/settings/business-details-and-time-zone).
