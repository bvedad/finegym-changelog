---
title: "Invoices Can Be Paid in Parts, and You See Who Still Owes What"
date: "2026-10-13"
categories: ["Admin", "Mobile", "Payments"]
---

A member who brings half of what they owe no longer leaves you choosing between an open invoice and a wrong one. Every invoice now keeps track of what has been paid and what is still due.

**Take any amount at the desk.** Choose **Record payment** on an invoice, lower the amount to what the member is paying today, and take it in cash, with one of your own payment methods, on a card they saved with you, or on a new card. The invoice shows **Paid** and **Amount due**, reads **Partially paid** until the last part is in, and is marked paid on its own when it is.

**Members pay the rest online.** On your gym site and in the app, a partly paid invoice shows what is left, and members pay it with a card they saved or a new one.

**Refund one payment, not the whole invoice.** Each payment has its own **Refund**, in full or in part, back to the card through Stripe or in cash. On a paid invoice, tick **The member still owes the refunded amount** to reopen it for that amount.

**See who still owes you.** The new **Outstanding Balances** report under **Reports** lists everyone with money left to pay, how much, and since when, with a CSV export. The invoice list has a **Partially paid** filter and an **Amount due** column, and receipts and payment emails tell the member what is still due.

Read more in the [partial payments guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/partial-payments).
