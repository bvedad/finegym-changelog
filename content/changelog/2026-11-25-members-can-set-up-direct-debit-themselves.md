---
title: "Members Can Set Up Direct Debit Themselves"
date: "2026-11-25"
categories: ["Payments", "Members", "Mobile"]
---

Until now, direct debit started with you: staff sent a member the link. Now your members can choose it on their own, on your website and in the member app.

**You decide.** **Settings → Bank connection** has a new switch in its **Direct debit** card: **Let members choose direct debit themselves**. It is off until you turn it on, and while it is off nothing changes.

**When they buy a plan.** A plan billed every week or month asks **How would you like to pay?**, with **Card** and **Bank account**. A member who chooses the bank account enters it on Stripe's secure form, and the membership starts at once. The first payment is on its way to you, and its invoice reads **Processing** until the money arrives.

**For a membership they already have.** The membership shows **Pay by direct debit**. The member taps **Set up direct debit**, enters the bank account on Stripe's form, and the membership is collected from it from then on, starting with any invoice still open.

**Also in this release**

- The **Transactions** report lists direct debit on a row of its own under **Payment methods**, named by its scheme (such as **ACH Direct Debit**), instead of counting it with cards.
- The member app gets this with its next update.

Read more in [Accept direct debit payments](https://docs.finegym.io/payments/accept-direct-debit-payments) and, for your members, [Pay by direct debit](https://docs.finegym.io/for-members/pay-by-direct-debit).
