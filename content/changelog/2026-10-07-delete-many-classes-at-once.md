---
title: "Delete Many Classes at Once"
date: "2026-10-07"
categories: ["Admin", "Scheduling"]
---

Classes could only be deleted one at a time. A repeating class created twice by mistake meant a year of duplicates and a year of clicking; clearing a week while the gym was closed meant the same. **Bulk delete** removes them in one go.

**Choose the classes, see what will happen, then delete.** On the **Classes** page, click **Bulk delete** and narrow down by class type, weekdays, start time, coach and a date range. Finegym shows how many classes match, how many bookings would be cancelled, and lists them before you confirm. Past and already-started classes are never touched, and you must choose a class type or an end date, so one click can never empty your whole schedule.

**Duplicates are a two-click job.** Open the duplicated class, choose **Bulk delete classes** from its menu, and the class type, start time and coach are already filled in: everything in that series from today on, nothing else.

**Members are looked after.** Bookings on deleted classes are cancelled the way staff would cancel them, so visits go back on the member's membership and waitlist entries are removed. Tick **Email and text members whose bookings are cancelled** to tell each of them automatically. Classes with recorded attendance are kept, so your attendance history stays intact; Finegym tells you how many it kept.

Read more in the [bulk deleting classes guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/bulk-deleting-classes).
