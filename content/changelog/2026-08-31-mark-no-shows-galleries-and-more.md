This release brings a handful of improvements across scheduling, products, memberships, and payments.

## Mark a no-show as present

Ran the register late, or did a member forget to check in? Staff and instructors can now **mark a past no-show as attended** — on a class, an appointment, or from the calendar. It's recorded on the correct day and counts as usage, with no time limit. Read more in the [class reservations guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/class-reservations#marking-a-no-show-as-present).

## Multiple product images

Products can now have a **gallery of images** instead of a single photo — upload several, drag to reorder, and the first image becomes the cover shown in your catalog and on invoices. Read more in the [product catalog guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/products-catalog#product-images).

## A cleaner membership plan page

Each membership plan now opens to a redesigned page split into two tabs: **Overview** — key stats plus the plan's full configuration, grouped into cards — and **Members**, everyone currently on the plan. See the [membership plans guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/membership-plan-types#viewing-a-plan).

## Activity at a glance for members and staff

Every member and staff profile now has its own **Activity** tab — a single timeline of everything that person has done, without filtering the account-wide log. Read more in the [activity log guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/settings/activity-log#per-member-and-per-staff-activity).

## Clearer Stripe onboarding

When your Stripe account still needs information before it can accept payments, the **Bank Connection** page now shows exactly what's required and lets you pick up onboarding right where you left off. See the [bank connection guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/settings/bank-connection#finishing-a-restricted-account).
