---
title: "Resend Welcome Emails & Set Member Passwords"
date: "2026-08-16"
categories: ["Members", "Admin"]
---

Helping a member who can't sign in is now a two-click job. The member's **Profile** tab has a new **Login access** section with two actions.

- **Resend welcome email** — Send a member a fresh login password and their check-in QR code, right from their profile. Handy when the original email landed in spam or their address previously bounced.
- **Set password** — Assign a member a password yourself and share it directly. For security, existing passwords are stored encrypted and can't be shown, so this lets you set a new one instead.

Both live under **Member Profile → Profile → Login access**.

Read more in the [managing members guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/members-adding#login-access).
