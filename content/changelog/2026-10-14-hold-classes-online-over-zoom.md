date: "2026-10-14"

Your schedule no longer stops at the gym door. Connect Zoom once, and classes can be held online with every link handled for you.

**One meeting per class, created for you.** Mark a class type as **Online class (Zoom)** and every class of that type gets its own Zoom meeting the moment it is scheduled — single classes and recurring schedules alike. Move the class and the meeting moves with it; delete it and the meeting goes too.

**Members get the link, nobody else does.** The booking confirmation email carries the join link, and on your gym site and in the member app the **Join the class** button appears 30 minutes before start — only for members with a booking.

**The coach gets the host link.** The class page in the dashboard shows **Start the Zoom meeting** to staff and the class's coach, and when the coach's email is a user on your Zoom account, they host the meeting themselves. Members can join before the host arrives, so nobody waits in a lobby.

**Connected in minutes.** Create a small Server-to-Server app in your own Zoom account — no Zoom review, it works immediately — and paste its three credentials under **Settings → Integrations → Zoom**.

Read more in the [Zoom integration guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/settings/integrations/zoom-integration).
