---
title: "Your Gym Site Gets a Home Page, and Your Own Domain"
date: "2026-11-01"
categories: ["Admin", "Members"]
---

Until now your Finegym site started at the schedule. It is now a website, with a front page you write, and it can live on a domain of your own.

**A home page you control.** Under **Settings → Gym site**, write a tagline and an introduction, add a cover photo and a gallery, and choose which of your classes, plans and coaches appear. Your opening hours sit in the footer with whether you are open right now, and the page ends with a **Have a question?** button that writes to you.

**Your colors and your logo.** The main color you set under Branding becomes the site's buttons and links, and your logo is the site's icon in the browser tab and on a phone's home screen.

**Your own domain.** On a paid plan, connect a domain such as `yourstudio.com` or `book.yourstudio.com`: add the DNS records it shows you where you bought the domain, and Finegym checks them, sets up the certificate and serves your site there. Links to your schedule, plans and account pages keep working on the Finegym address too.

**Members sign in from the site.** The header carries Sign in and Book a class, and signed-in members reach their account, bookings, invoices and documents from the same site.

Read more in [your website](https://docs.finegym.io/settings/your-website).
