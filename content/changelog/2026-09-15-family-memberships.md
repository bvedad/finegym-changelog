title: "Family Memberships: One Plan for a Parent and Their Kids"
date: "2026-09-15"

Martial-arts schools and family gyms have always had the same workaround for kids: give each child a membership of their own — usually a $0 one — just so they can be booked in and checked in. That meant fake plans, $0 invoices in the reports, and an email address invented for a seven-year-old.

Now a membership can simply be **shared with family**.

**Switch it on per plan.** Any membership plan gets a **Shareable with family** toggle. Everyone in the holder's family can check in and book classes and appointments on it, and they all draw on the same usage limit — a 10-session family card is 10 sessions between the lot of them.

**Children join as dependents, with just a name.** From a member's profile, create a family and **Add dependent**: full name, optional date of birth, done. A dependent is a real member — profile, QR code, PIN, attendance history — but has **no email and no login**. Anything emailed to them, from booking confirmations to your broadcasts, reaches the parent instead, and a family gets each broadcast once. Existing members can be linked too: a spouse, or a teenager with their own account.

**Book and check them in like anyone else.** Wherever staff pick a member, the memberships offered are the ones that *cover* them — so a child on a family plan is booked into a class, enrolled in a series or booked for an appointment exactly like an adult, and siblings can each take a place in the same class on the one membership. At the desk, the child checks in with their own code; the visit is recorded under the child and counted against the family plan. Eligibility is checked on the child — a blocked child stays blocked while the parent is fine — and freezing the parent's plan pauses the whole family.

**Parents run it themselves.** In the app and on your gym's site, **Family** lets a member start a family, add and edit dependents, and remove people. Whoever manages the family gets a **Your family** panel on every class: one row per relative with their state — *Not booked*, *Reserved*, *#2 in line* — and the one action that fits: **Book**, **Cancel**, **Check In**, **Join waitlist**, **Leave waitlist**. Booking yourself never takes anything away from the panel. Appointments ask **Who is it for?**. The **Reservations** and **Visits** lists show one person at a time, with a **You / Emma / Noah** picker at the top, so nobody turns up to a class meant for their child.

**Holding the plan and managing the family are separate.** The membership belongs to whoever bought it, as always; managing is a role you grant (**Make manager** on the Family card), and it is the only thing that lets someone act for the others — so a parent whose plan has lapsed, or who set the family up before buying, still runs the household and books the kids on the other parent's plan, while a teenager who buys their own plan does not.

**Staff see the whole picture.** **Members → Families** lists every family, who manages it and who is in it, and flags one that nobody manages. A dependent's Membership tab shows **Covered by a family membership**, naming the plan and who holds it. Dependents count as **Active** in the members list and in the *Has active membership* and *Membership plan* filters, so children never land in your win-back campaign.

Nothing to migrate: existing members and plans are untouched until you switch **Shareable with family** on.

Read more in the [family memberships guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/families).
