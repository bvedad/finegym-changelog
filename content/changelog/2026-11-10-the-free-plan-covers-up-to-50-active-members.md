---
title: "The Free Plan Covers up to 50 Active Members"
date: "2026-11-10"
categories: ["Plans"]
---

The Free plan now covers **up to 50 active members**. Everything else about it is unchanged: all core features, unlimited staff accounts, no card and no expiry.

**Who counts.** An active member is someone with a current membership, or who has booked or checked in within the last 30 days. Staff, leads and former members who have neither don't count, so importing an old member list adds nobody until those people get a membership or come in.

**What happens at 50.** Everyone already active keeps booking, paying and checking in, whatever the count. At 50, giving a membership to someone who isn't active yet is refused, in the admin and online, until a membership ends or someone stops coming. **My plan** shows your count against the limit, and the admin warns you from 40.

**If you are over 50 today.** Your members keep working as before, and we've emailed you with a month of Starter so you can move without a gap. Starter and Growth have no member limit.

Read more in [Plans](https://docs.finegym.io/settings/plans).
