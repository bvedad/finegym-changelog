---
title: "Unsaved Changes Protection"
date: "2026-08-23"
categories: ["Admin", "Improvements"]
---

The dashboard now protects your work: if you try to close a form or leave a page with unsaved changes, you'll get a confirmation first — so an accidental click no longer loses your edits.

- **Forms & panels** — Closing a form, pressing Escape, or clicking outside while you have unsaved changes now asks before discarding
- **Page navigation** — Moving to another page, using the browser back button, or refreshing with unsaved changes prompts you to confirm
