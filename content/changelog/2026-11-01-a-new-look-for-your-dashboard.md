title: "A New Look for Your Dashboard"
date: "2026-11-01"
categories: ["Admin", "Improvements"]
---

Every screen of the dashboard has been rebuilt. Nothing you rely on moved out of reach, but most of it is now quicker to find, lighter to load and easier to read.

**The sidebar is grouped by the job at hand.** Schedule holds the calendar, classes and appointments; People holds members, leads and marketing; Money holds payments, reports and expenses; Sell holds plans, products and discounts. Settings are grouped the same way, by what they set up.

**Search from anywhere.** Press **⌘K** (or **Ctrl+K**) and type a member's name, a plan or an invoice number. The same box sits at the top of every page.

**Lists do more with less clicking.** Pick a named period such as this week or last month instead of two dates, open **More filters** only when you need them, and choose the columns you see. Switching a list's view no longer throws away your filters.

**Today tells you how the business is doing.** Under the day's schedule you see sales, new members, visits and bookings for the last 7 days, 4 weeks or 3 months, each against the period before.

**It is faster.** The first download is less than half the size it was, so the dashboard opens quicker on a phone or a slow connection. Every page also passes an accessibility check, so it works with a keyboard and a screen reader.

**And it speaks plainly.** Finegym now calls your organization a business, not a gym, uses US English throughout, and writes labels in sentence case. Portuguese is Brazilian Portuguese everywhere.

The help center has been rewritten to match, screen by screen, at [docs.finegym.io](https://docs.finegym.io).
