Reach the right members at the right moment, by email, text message or app notification, without writing to each of them yourself. Marketing is part of the **Growth** plan.

**Automations that run on their own.** Start from a template, such as **Welcome new members**, **Win back absent members**, **Membership renewal reminder** or **Birthday greeting**, or build your own. Each automation starts with a trigger: a member joins, a membership starts, is canceled or is about to end, a member hasn't visited for a number of days, has a birthday, reaches a number of visits, or has few visits left on a punchcard. Then come the steps: emails, texts, app notifications, waits and conditions, laid out on a canvas you drag steps around on. Set a goal, such as checking in or becoming a member, and members leave the flow as soon as they reach it. Each automation shows its results, and who is in it right now.

**Campaigns for one-off news.** Send holiday hours, a new class or an offer to all your members or to a segment, right away or at a time you choose, as an email, a text or an app notification. Afterwards you see who got it, who opened it and who clicked.

**Segments.** Group members by membership status, plan, days since their last visit or days since they joined. Send a campaign to a segment, or split an automation by one.

**Respectful by default.** Every marketing email carries an unsubscribe link, and texts go only to members who said yes to them. Automations never send a member more than your weekly limit of messages, and no text or app notification goes out between 21:00 and 8:00 in your gym's time zone.

Read more in the [automations guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/marketing-automations) and the [campaigns guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/marketing-campaigns).
