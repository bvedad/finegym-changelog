title: "Printable Member Cards"
date: "2026-08-02"
categories: ["Admin", "Members"]
---

You can now design and print physical check-in cards for your members. Cards are standard bank-card size (CR80), so they fit any wallet and work with off-the-shelf PVC card printers. Customize everything under **Settings → Member Cards**.

- **Your branding** — Set the accent color and choose whether to show your gym name, logo, tagline, and a contact line
- **QR or barcode** — Print a QR code or a 1D barcode as the check-in code; both scan the same way at the front desk
- **Layout options** — Landscape or portrait, with an optional member PIN
- **Live preview** — See the card at its true printed size before printing, then print one member from their profile or many at once from the Members list

For more details, see the [Member Cards guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/settings/member-cards).
