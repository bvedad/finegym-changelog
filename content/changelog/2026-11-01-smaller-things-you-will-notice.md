date: "2026-11-01"

Alongside the new look, a long list of smaller changes shipped with this release. The ones you are most likely to meet:

**Your emails lead with your logo.** Every email a member gets from you, from a receipt to a campaign, opens with your logo or name, carries your color on its buttons, and closes with your address and links. The template editor writes inside the email, as the campaign editor does, and templates you have rewritten are marked **Customized**.

**Messages on the member profile.** Every email and text a member received is on its own **Messages** tab, with the automations they are in above it. Marketing consent moved to the Profile tab.

**Sign-up is quicker.** A new business is built in the background while the owner watches, instead of one long wait. The welcome email was rewritten around the first three steps to take.

**Refer a business.** Your referral link is under **Settings → Refer a business**. A business that signs up through it gets its first month on a paid plan free, and once it has paid for two months you get three months of your plan as credit on your next invoices.

**A note of when they joined.** A member's profile shows the day they joined, with the time when you hover.

**Changing your password asks for the current one.** Staff are asked for their current password when setting a new one.

**Fixes worth knowing.** Money shows in the business's currency format everywhere; overlapping classes sit side by side on the calendar; frozen memberships are left out when booking; a sold-out product says so; canceled memberships keep their last day; a coach is never double-booked; deleting a class cancels its bookings; paused automations resume where each member was; forms that could save the wrong record or run twice no longer can.
