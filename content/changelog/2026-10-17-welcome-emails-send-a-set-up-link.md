---
title: "Welcome Emails Send a Set-Up Link, Never a Password"
date: "2026-10-17"
categories: ["Admin", "Members", "Improvements"]
---

Finegym no longer emails anyone a password. New members and new staff now get a **Set up your account** link and choose their own password.

**Members choose their password on your gym site.** The link in the welcome email opens your gym site, where the member picks a password and is signed in straight away. The email still carries their check-in PIN and QR code. Staff get the same kind of link, which opens the dashboard. Nobody, you included, ever sees the password.

**Links that can't be reused.** A link works once, and expires after 7 days for members and 3 days for staff. Anyone with an expired link can ask for a new one with a single click, and it goes to the same inbox.

**See who hasn't set up yet.** The **Login access** section on member and staff profiles shows whether the person has set up their account, and **Send setup link** emails them a fresh link. A password someone already chose keeps working until they use the new link, so nobody is locked out.

**Old passwords removed.** Passwords that earlier welcome emails carried are blanked out of the communication history, and welcome emails you customized now carry the set-up link.

Read more in the [members guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/members-adding#login-access).
