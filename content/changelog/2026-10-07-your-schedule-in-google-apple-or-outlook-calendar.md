Coaches run their lives from a calendar app, and Finegym wasn't in it. Now it is, in both directions.

**Your Finegym schedule in your own calendar.** Open **Calendar** or **Appointments** and click **Sync calendar**. Each staff member gets a private calendar link with their classes and their appointments, and one click adds it to **Google Calendar**, **Outlook** or **Apple Calendar**. From then on the calendar app keeps it up to date on its own: new classes, changed times, deleted classes and new bookings all appear without anyone doing anything. Google refreshes subscribed calendars about once a day, Apple and Outlook about every hour. If a link is ever shared by mistake, **Reset link** retires it on the spot.

**One link for the whole gym.** Whoever manages the staff can pick any coach from the **Calendar for** menu to set up their subscription, or choose **Whole gym** for a single calendar with every class and every appointment, ideal for a shared front-desk screen.

**Busy in Google means not bookable in Finegym.** Connect your Google Calendar on the same page and the times you're busy there are taken out of your appointment availability within five minutes, so a member can't book you while you're at the dentist. Finegym reads only whether you're free or busy, never what your events are, and shows which Google account you connected so there's no guessing. Classes are a fixed timetable and aren't changed by your calendar; for those, delete the class or add a closure.

Read more in the [calendar sync guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/calendar-feed).
