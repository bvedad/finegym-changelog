title: "Staff Pay Rates and a Payroll Report"
date: "2026-10-14"
categories: ["Admin", "Reports"]
---

What you owe your team no longer lives in a spreadsheet next to Finegym. Give each staff member a pay rate, and the new **Payroll** report works out their earnings from the classes and appointments they actually taught.

**Set a rate once, on the staff profile.** A flat amount per class, per appointment, or per hour for sessions without a flat rate, plus an optional bonus per booked attendee. Class types and appointment types that pay differently get their own override, and a toggle decides whether an empty class still earns its rate.

**Read the period at a glance.** The **Payroll** report under **Reports** shows one row per staff member — classes, appointments, hours, attendees and pay — and each row unfolds into the sessions behind it, with the rate every one was paid at. Cancelled sessions never count, and someone taught without a rate shows up with zero pay rather than disappearing.

**Close the period once it is settled.** Closing freezes the numbers exactly as shown, so a rate changed next week or a corrected class no longer moves what you already paid out. Reopen it at any time to compute live again, and **Export CSV** hands the period to your accountant.

Read more in the [staff pay rates guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/staff-pay-rates).
