A full class used to be a dead end. Members who wanted in had to keep checking back, and when someone cancelled, the freed spot usually went unnoticed — so classes ran with empty places while people would happily have taken them.

Now they can queue. **Any class type with an attendance limit gets a waitlist automatically** — there is nothing to switch on.

**For members**, in the app: tap **Join waitlist** on a full class and your place is shown right there — `#2 in line`. If someone cancels, the first person in the queue is **booked in automatically and emailed**. There is no spot to claim and no link to race for. Joining costs nothing: no session or punch is used while you wait, only when you are actually booked in.

**For staff**, on the class page: a **Waitlist** card shows everyone in line, in order, with their plan and when they joined — and you can remove anyone. The attendee count now reads `8 / 8 +3 waiting`, so a full class explains itself at a glance. On a full class, **Add attendee** becomes **Add to waitlist**, so the action in front of you is always one that can actually work.

Spots open in more ways than a cancellation, and all of them promote from the queue: **raise a class type's attendance limit** and the waiting members are let in; **remove the limit entirely** and everyone waiting gets a place.

One detail worth knowing: a member's membership is checked when a spot opens, not when they joined. If it expired or ran out of sessions while they waited, the spot passes to the next person — but **they keep their place in the queue** and get one email explaining why, so they can sort it out before the next spot comes up.

All four waitlist emails — joined, booked in, passed over, removed — can be edited or switched off in **Settings → Email Templates**.

Read more in the [class reservations guide](https://docs.finegym.io/admin/class-reservations#waitlists).
